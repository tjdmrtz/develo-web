Marketplace and Ecommerce Automation with AI

Selling on marketplaces means thousands of repetitive buyer questions: shipping times, compatibility, order status, returns. Develo builds AI agents that answer them with your live catalog and order data — protecting your seller rating and freeing your team for high-value work.

What we automate

Buyer questions

Product info, stock, prices, shipping and returns answered instantly, 24/7.

Order status & logistics

Live answers backed by your orders and tracking data.

Returns management

Policy-compliant guidance that reduces disputes and chargebacks.

Seller operations

Automated routines for listings, pricing, inventory sync and reporting.

Why AI instead of canned replies

Marketplace buyers ask the same question in a hundred ways, and they expect an accurate answer in seconds. Rule-based auto-replies fail on the variants; AI agents with access to your catalog and orders don't. The result: faster response times (a ranking factor on marketplaces), higher conversion and a better seller reputation.
